## Initial Lab Zero fit check

Potential thematic fit: systemic barriers to women’s leadership and economic participation through equitable access to AI, robotics, technical education, mentorship, and career pathways.

Confirm before doing application work:

1. The applicant is a legally constituted Canadian nonprofit.
2. The organization can document prior experience advancing women’s equality.
3. The proposal addresses systemic change rather than only delivering a one-off program.
4. The appropriate scope is local (up to $625,000), regional (up to $1 million), or pan-Canadian (up to $1.4 million).
5. No other application is being submitted by the same organization.
6. Leadership has authority to make the representations and commitments required by the portal.

Women’s and Indigenous women’s organizations are prioritized. Do not submit or invent organizational evidence without authorized source documents.
